Contrast

Generated by scripts/build.mjs: every pair the system relies on, in both themes, measured with the WCAG 2 contrast formula. Translucent colours are blended over their ground in sRGB, as browsers do; a translucent ground is measured on both the page and the surface, and the worse counts. Text needs 4.5:1; controls, focus and chart marks need 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11). Hairlines (line, line-strong) and the pattern are decorative, and not listed.
