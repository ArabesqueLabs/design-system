Motion

Motion only ever answers something a person did: a disclosure opening, a toast arriving, a theme changing. Nothing moves by itself, loops (except a spinner while something is busy) or plays on load, with one exception: the hero's pattern may draw itself in, once (below).

Token Value For
duration-fast 150ms Feedback: hover, press, focus, a field's state
duration-base 250ms Disclosure, a toast entering, theme change, sidebar
duration-slow 400ms Large surfaces only (a dialog's backdrop); rare
ease-standard cubic-bezier(.2, 0, 0, 1) Things arriving or changing
ease-exit cubic-bezier(.4, 0, 1, 1) Things leaving

Rules

  • Short and purposeful. If a person waits on an animation, it's too long.
  • Animate opacity and transform, so motion is cheap and never shifts layout. (The skeleton's shimmer, a background position, is the one exception, and it stops under reduced motion.)
  • Reduced motion means none. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, css/base.css brings every transition and animation to an instant; the spinner becomes a still dotted ring; skeletons stop shimmering; nothing scrolls smoothly.
  • The pattern never moves, with one exception. As texture, it's never animated. The hero (drawHero with animate: true) may draw itself in once when the page loads, the way it's constructed: the tiling as scaffolding, the strands from the focal tile outwards, then the scaffolding fades and the focal tile appears, in under three seconds. Never again on that page, never in a loop, and never under reduced motion.
  • Direction-aware. Anything that moves sideways mirrors in right-to-left; a toast rises into place, which reads the same in both directions.
